My toolkit

The book's instruction: after working through all the exercises, choose favorites, then limit your practice to that set for a few months, until each one is automatic enough to reach for in any situation. Add new exercises one at a time; drop what doesn't earn its place.

Empty so far Browse the pivot pages and press "+ Add to my toolkit" on the exercises that fit you. A good starter kit covers all six pivots with one or two exercises each.

Daily rhythm (from the book)

  • Anchor short practices to routine moments: waking, shower, breakfast, midday, evening.
  • Use the "practiced today" checkboxes on exercise pages; they reset every day.
  • When a thought, feeling or urge hooks you mid-day, reach for a toolkit exercise on the spot.
  • Do one new thing daily that requires openness and presence and touches your values.
  • Do one kind thing daily for someone, anonymously if you like.
